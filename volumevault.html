VolumeVault — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Pixelvault Labs
Last updated: 27 September 2026

VolumeVault is built on a simple principle: your audio stays yours.

Data collection: none

  • VolumeVault does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.
  • Audio is processed entirely on your device using the Web Audio API. Nothing is recorded, uploaded, or sent anywhere. VolumeVault makes zero network requests.
  • The only thing stored is your preferred volume level per website (chrome.storage.local, on your device only) so it can be remembered between visits.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — to route the current tab's audio through a local gain node when you click "Boost this tab". Used only on your explicit action.
  • storage — remembers your per-site volume levels locally.
