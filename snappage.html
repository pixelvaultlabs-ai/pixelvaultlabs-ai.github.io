SnapPage — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Pixelvault Labs
Last updated: 27 September 2026

SnapPage is built on a simple principle: your browsing is yours.

Data collection: none

  • SnapPage does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.
  • Screenshots are captured and stitched entirely on your device. The resulting PNG is downloaded directly to your computer via the browser's download manager.
  • No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party requests of any kind. SnapPage makes no external network requests — it works fully offline.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — to scroll the current page and stitch the capture. Used only when you click "Capture full page".
  • downloads — to save the PNG to your computer.
  • storage — reserved for future preferences; nothing is stored in v1.0.0.
  • Optional <all_urls> — only if you enable "Always allow on all sites"; this merely skips the per-site permission prompt. It changes nothing about data handling: still zero external network, zero collection.
